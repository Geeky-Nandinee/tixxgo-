2. Task 9 Deduplication & Scoring Formula

When the same flight (e.g. Air India AI101 AMD → DEL) is returned by both TBO (INR 8,450) and TripJack (INR 8,150), Tixxgo computes a multi-factor score:

Price Factor (35%)
Favors lower customer total (TripJack 8,150 wins +300 INR benefit).
Margin Factor (20%)
Negotiated commission (TripJack 4.5% vs TBO 3.0%).
Reliability SLA (20%)
Historical PNR confirmation rate (TBO 98.5% vs TripJack 96.2%).
Baggage Allowance (15%)
Check-in baggage (TripJack provides 20 KG vs TBO 15 KG).
